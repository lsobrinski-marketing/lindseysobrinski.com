One topic in. A prompt map out, with the engine that owns each moment.

Keyword lists were built for a ranked page of links. Buyers now ask questions, and three different engines answer them at three different points in the journey. This turns a topic into prompt clusters, assigns each cluster to the engine that actually rewards it, and tells you the asset to build and the schema it needs.

Each engine plays a different position in the journey

One "AI SEO playbook" is a mistake. The map below assigns every prompt to the engine that owns that moment.

Awareness · Top of Funnel

ChatGPT

Rewards citations and third-party corroboration. Open category questions, where your brand gets introduced. Think impressions, not clicks.

Consideration · Mid / Bottom Funnel

Gemini + AI Overviews

Rewards entity authority and surfaces brand mentions. Branded, comparison, and shortlist prompts get decided inside the answer box.

Research · Validation

Perplexity

Dense citations, sources on display. Mid-funnel research and proof-seeking. Gated expertise does not exist here.

Nothing leaves this page, the map is generated in your browser. The prompt patterns are the ones buyers actually type; the engine assignment follows where each engine sits in the journey, not a guess at any single model's ranking algorithm. Use it as the content plan, then measure share of answer per engine against it.
