Recovery rates are the defaults I work from before a business has its own numbers: roughly 45% of involuntary churn is recoverable with competent dunning and card-updater coverage, 15% of never-activated customers with a real onboarding intervention, 5% of completed customers through a genuinely different offer, and 10% of dissatisfied customers where the underlying cause gets fixed rather than discounted. The capability slider scales all four. Every one of these should be replaced with a measured number from a holdout as soon as you have one, because a recovery program without a control group reports the customers who were coming back anyway as saves.
