Eric Brinker is the founder of Brinker Analytics, an independent marketing measurement consultancy helping DTC brands prove which ad channels actually drive incremental sales. He has personally launched $123M in digital campaigns, directed about $60M a year in department ad spend, and led 70+ incrementality tests. He writes about holdout testing, marketing mix modeling, and practical ad measurement for founders and marketing leaders.

Eric has spent his career close to the budget decision: in the media accounts, in the reporting, and building the measurement systems a team needs when those two things disagree. Over more than a decade in DTC marketing analytics, he built an in-house measurement practice from scratch at a large direct-to-consumer brand while staying hands-on with the media buying it was meant to inform.

His work spans the full measurement stack: GA4 and tag management, identity resolution, BigQuery and Cloud Run data pipelines, attribution and sales reconciliation, and Tableau reporting, plus the shared definitions that let marketing, finance, and leadership work from the same numbers. The aim is never a better-looking dashboard. It is a channel-level spend decision with a clearer account of what the media actually added.

Before the measurement work, Eric worked on startup teams in publishing and marketing and founded Brinker Publishing, a small explainer-video studio serving enterprise and smaller clients. Today, Brinker Analytics is his independent practice, where he takes a small number of focused engagements and owns the work end to end. He is most useful when a question crosses boundaries: a media decision, a test, a data problem, and a definition fight, with an answer needed next week.
